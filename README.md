# Fareed (فريد) — kids SEL mobile app

Expo SDK 58 · React Native · Expo Router · EN/AR. Based on Sanaa Issa's Metanoia "Fareed" pitch.

## Run on a phone (Expo Go)
    cd ~/Desktop/projects/Fareed-Mobile
    npx expo start --port 8083
Scan the QR code with the iPhone camera (Expo Go installed, same Wi-Fi).
Browser preview: press `w` (or `npx expo start --web --port 8083`).

## What's inside
- **Fareed** (`src/components/Fareed.tsx`): vector panda in a keffiyeh + agal with a backpack, rigged at
  neck / shoulders / hips. Actions: idle, wave, jump, celebrate, run, dance, spin, talk, listen, think,
  sad, angry, scared, surprised, breatheIn/Out, nod, shake, freeze. 10 facial moods + blinking.
- **Home**: onboarding (Fareed runs in), mood check-in he mirrors, tap-Fareed tricks, levels and stars,
  daily kindness mission, and the **Jordan Journey map** — Fareed runs to each stop:
  - Amman · Feelings Charades — Fareed acts a feeling / a short scenario, child names it
  - Jerash · Music Theatre — Echo the Beat (memory), Feel the Music (emotion in sound), Freeze Dance (impulse control)
  - Dead Sea · Float & Breathe — balloon breathing (4-2-5) with sound, then re-check mood
  - Petra · Draw the Rose City — finger painting with feeling colours, prompts, gallery
  - Wadi Rum · Echo Canyon — speaking challenges + "I feel… when… I need…" builder, record & hear your echo
  - Aqaba · Kindness Reef — choose-your-path stories acted by Fareed, read aloud
- **Grown-ups** (cog, maths gate): 5 CASEL skill bars, mood log, activity counts, language, reset.
- Sounds are generated by `tools/make_sounds.py` (no copyrighted audio).
- Progress is saved on the device (AsyncStorage `fareed-mobile-v1`).

## Games (interactive, one per place + extras)
| Place | Game | Mechanic | CASEL skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amman | Feelings Catch | drag Fareed to catch the feeling that fits a situation | Self- & social awareness |
| Amman | Feelings Map | place feeling words on an energy × pleasant grid (Russell 1980) | Self-awareness |
| Jerash | Beat Theatre | 4-lane rhythm game; timing lifts Fareed's mood | Self-management (EF) |
| Dead Sea | Balloon Float | hold = breathe in, release = out; stars on a 6 breaths/min wave | Self-management |
| Petra | Mood Monster Maker | build eyes/brows/mouth to show a feeling | Self-awareness |
| Wadi Rum | Brave Voice Run | runner; say a brave word out loud (mic metering) or tap to jump | Relationship skills |
| Aqaba | Kindness Reef | swim to collect kind acts, dodge unkind jellyfish | Social awareness |
| Aqaba | Stop · Think · Go | red/green light running + problem-solving choices with consequences | Responsible decisions |

## Curriculum
`src/lib/curriculum.ts` — 5 units (one per CASEL competency, sequenced: name feelings → calm → others' feelings →
relationships → problem-solving), 20 sessions of 10–15 min (2–3/week, ~8 weeks), SAFE design (Durlak et al. 2011),
repeated practice at rising difficulty (Diamond & Lee 2011), a home mission per unit, "Why this works" notes and
APA references with honest limits. Screens: `/path` (Learning Path, sessions unlock in order and complete
from real activity counts) and `/research` (CASEL definitions, SAFE, evidence, references).
